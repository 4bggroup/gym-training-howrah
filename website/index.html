08 / BEFORE YOUR FIRST REP

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

Starting something new should feel a little easier.

I’ve never trained in a gym. Where do I start?

Start with a conversation about your experience and goals. Personal coaching is a useful way to learn the equipment, practise the basics and find a pace you feel comfortable with.

Is LOHAA for both women and men?

Yes. LOHAA’s training approach is for adults of all genders and experience levels. You don’t need to arrive already strong or confident.

Do I need to choose one training style?

No. Strength, cardio and mobility can all have a place in your routine. Your starting point can change as you gain experience and understand what you enjoy.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Wear comfortable training clothes and supportive shoes. Bring a water bottle, a small towel and any questions you want to discuss.

How do I find out about fees and timings?

Confirm current membership fees, session availability and opening hours directly with the studio before making a visit or payment.

Can I come back after a long break?

Absolutely. Tell your coach about your recent activity, any limitations and what you want from training. Give yourself room to rebuild steadily.
