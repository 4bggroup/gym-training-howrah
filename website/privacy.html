ENQUIRY PRIVACY

This notice explains how the website’s enquiry form handles your information.

What the form collects

Your name, mobile number, area, training goal, preferred contact time, optional email and message, and your agreement to be contacted. Please do not include medical records or other sensitive information in your message.

When the enquiry service is connected

Your enquiry is sent to this website’s server and stored so the studio can respond. A reference is shown only after the server confirms that it saved your request. Submission does not confirm an appointment.

When you are viewing a preview

The form says “Preview mode”. Your information remains in the page until you download a text copy or close it. It is not submitted. A downloaded copy remains on your own device until you delete it.

Analytics and advertising

This package includes no advertising trackers or analytics services. Browser form autofill may operate according to your browser settings.

Questions or removal requests

Ask the studio through its published business contact details about your enquiry or request removal of your record. The business owner must provide those details before opening this enquiry service to the public.
